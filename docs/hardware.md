# Hardware Architecture

PocketCam Classique V1 is built around an ESP32-CAM with an OV5640
camera module.

Contents:
- ESP32-CAM
- OV5640 camera (attached to the ESP32CAM)
- 1.3" 240×240 ST7789 TFT display
- microSD card
- 3.7V LiPo battery
- IP5310 boost module
- master power switch (on/off sliding toggle switch)
- shutter button (tactile switch)

Development:
 Breadboard and jumper cables will be used for the prototype camera before soldering anything and putting the contents in the final enclosure.
